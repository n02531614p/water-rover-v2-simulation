Power distributionsheet 1 / 4

Two USB outputs from the power bank: one powers the ESP32 by its USB cable, the other is the 5 V rail for the motors, pumps, servo and flow sensor. Every ground is joined.

Drive (#9)sheet 2 / 4

The four TT motors run in pairs: both left motors in parallel on OUT1/OUT2, both right motors on OUT3/OUT4. ENA and ENB carry PWM from the ESP32, IN1-IN4 set direction.

Relays and pumps (#1 #3 #8 #10)sheet 3 / 4

The four relays switch the four pumps, never the motors. Each relay passes the 5 V rail from COM to NO and on to its pump; the pump returns to ground.

Servo, flow sensor, battery sense (#5 #6 #10)sheet 4 / 4

The servo signal and the flow-sensor pulse are the only other ESP32 inputs and outputs. Both sensors that feed analog or 5 V signals into the ESP32 go through dividers.

Bring-up checklist

  1. Power bank off. Wire every GND to one common bus first.
  2. L298N: remove the ENA, ENB and 5V-EN jumpers; +12V and +5V both on the 5 V rail.
  3. Relay board: remove the VCC to JD-VCC jumper; VCC to ESP32 3V3, JD-VCC to the 5 V rail.
  4. Fit a flyback diode across each pump (stripe to +) and the 1000 uF cap across the rail.
  5. Flow sensor yellow wire through the 10k / 20k divider to GPIO34, never straight in.
  6. ESP32 on its own USB output. Flash, open the Serial Monitor at 115200, check the boot line.
  7. Switch on the motor rail. Tap Forward on the dashboard: both sides must turn forward.
  8. Run each pump from the dashboard (intake, dose alum 1 mL, dose chlorine 0.5 mL, deliver 50 mL).
